# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

This policy covers Trizbort: Recast and Trizbort (the legacy Windows app), the free, open-source map-making apps
for interactive fiction published at <https://github.com/leeoades/trizbort>.

## The short version

Trizbort does not collect, store or send any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
telemetry, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your computer

Everything Trizbort saves is kept on your own computer, in places you choose or in its settings folder:

- **Your maps**, saved where you save them.
- **Settings and recent files**: your preferences and the list of maps you opened recently, in Trizbort's settings
  folder.
- **Recovery copies** of open maps, so your work can be restored if the app closes unexpectedly.
- **Error logs**, written only if the app runs into a problem. A log may include the map or game transcript you were
  working on, to help find the cause. It stays on your computer and is never sent anywhere. You can choose to attach
  one to a bug report yourself.
- **Game transcripts** that automap reads. Trizbort only reads these; the game or interpreter you play in writes
  them.

You can delete any of these files whenever you like.

## When Trizbort uses the internet

Trizbort only goes online when you ask it to:

- **Load from Web** downloads a map from the address you type in. The website you download from sees an ordinary
  request from your computer, as it would from a web browser, and its own privacy policy applies.
- **Links** in the app (Help, the About box) open in your web browser.

Trizbort does not check for updates or contact any server on its own.

## Downloads

Trizbort is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub's own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
covers your visit to GitHub and the download.

## Code signing

Windows releases may be digitally signed so you can check they came from this project unchanged. Signing happens
when a release is built and has nothing to do with you or your computer; running a signed app sends nothing
anywhere.

## Children

Trizbort collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. The history of every change is
in this repository.

## Questions

Please ask by opening an issue at <https://github.com/leeoades/trizbort/issues>.
