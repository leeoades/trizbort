What's new¶

Back to the guide

The highlights of each release, newest first. Every change is listed in the changelog.

Coming next¶

  • Backgrounds: coloured shapes behind parts of the map, to set them apart. Select some rooms and press B. See Drawing the map.

  • Add New Region... on a room's Set Region menu makes a region for the selected rooms, and the default region is called No Region.
  • Directions know a magic word back once you've used it both ways ("xyzzy" there and back).
  • Automap's guesses read more plainly: an untried way is "unexplored", a way that comes back says it looks like a loop, and Wrong Room: Choose... shows a new room beside the room you left.
  • Assume joins go both ways is the new name for "Always assume two-way connections", and each option in the automapping dialog has an ⓘ to hover over for more about it.
  • A shorter automapping dialog. The game screen is shown or hidden on the automap panel, and join labels follow the map's Direction words setting.

0.15 (22 November 2026)¶

You are here. The room automap has you in carries a token, like a playing piece on a board, and glows. Drag the token to put automap right. Choose your token in App Settings, or load your own.

Markdown labels. Headings, bold, lists, tables, and links to rooms you can click. A toolbar puts the marks in for you, and the label grows to fit.

Automap says when it guessed. When it picks a room for you and it could be another, the panel says what it guessed and why, with Wrong Room: Choose... to fix it.

Also: Unreliable joins for ways that don't always lead the same place, and Split Room now draws both rooms as the map does.

0.14 (16 November 2026)¶

Directions, like a satnav: right-click a room and choose Navigate to Here. The route glows on the map, and follows you as you play.

Also: Automap Settings can be changed while automap runs (the ⚙ on the panel), Play to the End shows its progress, and each tidy is an undo step of its own.

0.13 (15 November 2026)¶

The map rethinks itself. When a move shows the map was laid out the wrong way round, automap lays it out again, and the rooms slide to their new places. Layouts are tidier and better balanced, and rooms you placed yourself stay put.

Split Room... for a room that turns out to be two places with one name, and a bigger game screen in the automap panel.

0.12 (10 November 2026)¶

The automap panel gathers automap's controls beside the map: where you are, the transcript, and mapping or tracking only.

Also: rooms that move (lifts, cars), Merge Rooms, Split Join to tidy away long joins, darkness, and Which room does it mean? showing the room you pick on the map behind it.

0.11 (8 November 2026)¶

Renamed Trizbort: Recast. Rooms tidy themselves as you explore, and Tidy Map... tidies the whole map. Play a transcript move by move, and watch the game on a small game screen.

0.10 (2 November 2026)¶

Recovery copies of your unsaved work after a crash, and plainer error messages. Automap follows magic words (xyzzy) and lists several ways between two rooms on one join.

0.9 (31 October 2026)¶

The first beta of Recast: the same app on Windows, Linux and macOS. Undo and redo for every change, trackpads and touchscreens, and sharp, searchable PDF export.

Before Recast¶

Trizbort 2 added map labels, curved joins and themes to the original Windows app. See the original guide.
